## Question

Which checks establish that this simulator is ready to build and accept? Set target device and Android version, offline installation, ranking fixtures and ties, conservation of 52 unique cards, burn/runout order, playback interruption and backgrounding, readable results, and animation performance. Write the final build-ready specification from settled decisions and confirm shared understanding before implementation.

Following the user-requested betting extension, also cover action order, chip conservation, folded-player eligibility, pot splitting, and clear per-deal stack resets.
