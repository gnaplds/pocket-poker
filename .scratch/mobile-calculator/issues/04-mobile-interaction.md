## Question

Scope closure on 2026-11-05: superseded by [the simulator map](../../mobile-simulator/map.md). This calculator-focused prototype is no longer required; no prototype was delivered. The original question follows for historical context.

Which phone layout and card-entry flow let the user enter, correct, and compare 2–6 known hands without losing track of the board or results? Review a rough prototype with the user. Cover adding and removing players, selected-card conflicts, board editing, calculation feedback, reset, readable suit distinctions, and touch accessibility. The prototype is a discussion artifact, not production implementation.

Also cover the accepted practice deal: generated known hands, stage-by-stage reveal, changing between manual entry and practice, and starting a fresh deal. Decide how best-five-card and winning-hand explanations fit the screen; those display details are not yet approved.
